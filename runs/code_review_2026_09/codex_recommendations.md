## Item 2 — D58 grouping construction

**Recommendation:** **Fold into Item 1(B)** and make valid joint group uncertainty an acceptance condition for the entire corrected set.

**Why:** Correct marginal variances cannot be summed as though conditioned components were independent. The ledger distinguishes the existing roughly sevenfold overstatement of Believer/Moderate SD from the roughly hundredfold overstatement that would result from summing the corrected marginals independently. Thus a marginal-only fix would make those grouped panels worse. The decisive identity is that assigning every component to truth must reproduce the full latent posterior. (`experiments/bistar_debias_mauna_loa.py:83–112`; `O:258–275`; `runs/code_review_2026_09/codex_review.md:38–46`.)

**Before / after:** Before, grouping drops cross-covariance and the stored decomposition lacks the conditional moments needed to recover it. After, for each retained hyperparameter draw, sum the selected group's kernel blocks and condition using the Cholesky factor of the **entire fitted additive training covariance plus observation noise**. Do not fit a group-only training covariance. Form each group's mixture from its own conditional mean/variance rows, then take its quantiles; neither adding component interval endpoints nor adding marginal variances is valid. Apply this symmetrically to truth and removed-bias groups. (`O:272–275`; `bistar_gp/decompose.py:49–60,69–75,92–101`; `experiments/toy_debias_demo.py:404–435`.)

**Implications and costs:** Reuse Item 1's per-draw factorization and calculate all displayed groups in that pass. Preserve aligned draw identities across components and groups. The existing MAP full-sum calculation is a useful reference, while tests must also cover between-draw covariance. Publish no supposedly corrected grouped card until the all-truth identity holds for means, total variance, and mixture quantiles. Records, original byte preservation, and hand-off are shared with Item 1, not a second D58 fit. (`bistar_gp/debias.py:70–84`; `O:277–286`.)

**Risks and what would change my mind:** Accidental group-only conditioning, independently permuted component rows, or double-added observation noise can produce plausible bands.
